Poster Design Project

Project Overview

This project involved designing an engaging social media poster. The goal was to create a visually compelling design with vibrant colors, bold typography, and balanced layouts. The poster is tailored to grab attention and communicate a message effectively.

Design Process

The design process included:

  • Brainstorming ideas and concepts.
  • Using Adobe Photoshop tools such as clipping masks, healing tools, and camera raw filters.
  • Experimenting with typography and layout to achieve visual harmony.

Final Design

The final design received positive feedback for its creativity and professionalism. It successfully met the goals of the mock social media campaign.
